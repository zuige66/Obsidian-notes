# 六级作文高级词汇

## 副词

### 至关重要的

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | important | 重要的 | It is important to learn English. |
| 高级 | **crucially** | 关键地；至关重要地 | **Crucially**, we need to improve our skills. |
| 高级 | **vitally** | 极其重要地 | This decision is **vitally** important. |
| 高级 | **significantly** | 重要地；显著地 | The project is **significantly** important. |
| 高级 | **critically** | 批判地；关键地 | **Critically** speaking, we need change. |
| 高级 | **essentially** | 本质上；必不可少地 | Air is **essentially** important for life. |
| 高级 | **indispensably** | 必不可少地 | Water is **indispensably** important. |

### 无可替代的

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | necessary | 必要的 | Learning is necessary for growth. |
| 高级 | **irreplaceable** | 不可替代的 | This experience is **irreplaceable**. |
| 高级 | **indispensable** | 必不可少的 | Reading is **indispensable** to learning. |
| 高级 | **unparalleled** | 无与伦比的；无可比拟的 | China has **unparalleled** growth. |
| 高级 | **incomparable** | 无可比拟的 | The beauty is **incomparable**. |
| 高级 | **matchless** | 无比的；无敌的 | Her talent is **matchless**. |
| 高级 | **unequaled** | 不相等的；无敌的 | This is a task of **unequaled** difficulty. |

### 完全的/极其

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | very | 非常 | It is very important. |
| 高级 | **extremely** | 极其地 | This is **extremely** important. |
| 高级 | **utterly** | 完全地；彻底地 | The situation is **utterly** different. |
| 高级 | **completely** | 完全地 | I **completely** agree with you. |
| 高级 | **absolutely** | 绝对地；完全地 | This is **absolutely** necessary. |
| 高级 | **totally** | 完全地；全部地 | I **totally** support this idea. |
| 高级 | **entirely** | 完全地；全部地 | This is **entirely** true. |
| 高级 | **thoroughly** | 彻底地；完全地 | We must **thoroughly** understand it. |
| 高级 | **perfectly** | 完全地；完美地 | It is **perfectly** clear. |
| 高级 | **profoundly** | 深刻地；极大地 | This influenced me **profoundly**. |
| 高级 | **remarkably** | 显著地；非常 | She is **remarkably** talented. |

### 越来越多

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | more and more | 越来越多 | There are more and more people. |
| 高级 | **increasingly** | 越来越多地 | The problem is **increasingly** serious. |
| 高级 | **progressively** | 逐步地；渐进地 | Things are **progressively** improving. |
| 高级 | **gradually** | 渐渐地；逐步地 | The situation is **gradually** changing. |
| 高级 | **steadily** | 稳定地；持续地 | The economy is **steadily** growing. |
| 高级 | **continuously** | 连续不断地 | The world is **continuously** evolving. |
| 高级 | **constantly** | 不断地；持续地 | Technology is **constantly** improving. |

### 事实上/实际上

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | in fact | 事实上 | In fact, I agree with you. |
| 高级 | **actually** | 实际上 | **Actually**, this is quite different. |
| 高级 | **essentially** | 本质上 | **Essentially**, we are the same. |
| 高级 | **practically** | 实际上；事实上 | **Practically** speaking, it works. |
| 高级 | **virtually** | 实际上；几乎 | It is **virtually** impossible. |
| 高级 | **basically** | 基本上 | **Basically**, I support this idea. |
| 高级 | **fundamentally** | 根本上 | This changed things **fundamentally**. |
| 高级 | **intrinsically** | 本质上；内在地 | This is **intrinsically** valuable. |

### 此外/另外

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | also | 也 | I also think so. |
| 高级 | **furthermore** | 此外；而且 | The plan is good. **Furthermore**, it's feasible. |
| 高级 | **moreover** | 此外；再者 | It's cheap. **Moreover**, it's effective. |
| 高级 | **additionally** | 此外；另外 | **Additionally**, we need time. |
| 高级 | **besides** | 此外 | **Besides**, it's interesting. |
| 高级 | **likewise** | 同样地 | I agree. **Likewise**, others do too. |
| 高级 | **similarly** | 同样地 | **Similarly**, we should try harder. |

### 然而/但是

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | but | 但是 | I agree, but I have concerns. |
| 高级 | **however** | 然而 | I agree. **However**, there are problems. |
| 高级 | **nevertheless** | 然而；不过 | The task is hard. **Nevertheless**, we try. |
| 高级 | **nonetheless** | 然而；尽管如此 | It's expensive. **Nonetheless**, we buy it. |
| 高级 | **otherwise** | 否则；不然 | Study hard; **otherwise**, you fail. |
| 高级 | **whereas** | 然而；鉴于 | Some agree, **whereas** others disagree. |
| 高级 | **conversely** | 相反地 | Not small, but **conversely**, very big. |

### 因此/所以

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | so | 所以 | I studied hard, so I passed. |
| 高级 | **therefore** | 因此 | It is raining; **therefore**, we stay home. |
| 高级 | **thus** | 因此；从而 | She studied hard; **thus**, she succeeded. |
| 高级 | **consequently** | 因此；结果 | He was late; **consequently**, he missed the bus. |
| 高级 | **accordingly** | 因此；相应地 | The demand grew; **accordingly**, we increased supply. |
| 高级 | **hence** | 因此；所以 | The weather is bad; **hence**, we cancel. |
| 高级 | **as a result** | 结果 | He worked hard; **as a result**, he succeeded. |

## 动词

### 认为/视为

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | think | 认为 | I think you are right. |
| 基础 | consider | 考虑；认为 | I consider this important. |
| 高级 | **deem** | 认为；视为 | This action is **deemed** necessary. |
| 高级 | **regard** | 认为；把...看作 | He is **regarded** as an expert. |
| 高级 | **view** | 看待；认为 | They **view** this as a challenge. |
| 高级 | **perceive** | 感知；认为 | We **perceive** it differently. |
| 高级 | **conceive** | 构想；认为 | I **conceive** it as an opportunity. |

### 导致/促使

| 级别 | 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 基础 | cause | 导致；引起 | This **caused** the problem. |
| 基础 | lead to | 导致 | This **leads to** success. |
| 高级 | **drive** | 导致；驱动 | Technology is **driving** change. |
| 高级 | **propel** | 推进；促使 | Innovation **propels** growth. |
| 高级 | **trigger** | 触发；引起 | Stress can **trigger** illness. |
| 高级 | **induce** | 诱发；导致 | The drug **induced** sleep. |
| 高级 | **prompt** | 促使；引起 | The crisis **prompted** action. |
| 高级 | **precipitate** | 加速；促使 | The event **precipitated** change. |
